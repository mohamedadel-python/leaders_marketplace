## UAT
1. Marketplace > Configuration > Delivery Zones: add a row, tick Platform, governorate Cairo, price 40 (vendor field disappears). Vendor rows unchanged; filter "Platform" / "Vendors".
2. Website: cart with a company product (+ optionally a vendor FBM product) shipped to Cairo with the "Marketplace Delivery (Per Governorate)" carrier: total shows Delivery = 40 (+ vendor price) and under it "Leaders delivery: 40 / <Store> delivery: 30".
3. Ship to a governorate with no platform price with a company/FBL product: the carrier is not offered / error "We do not deliver to <governorate>."
4. Set a threshold (platform row, or a vendor's row in backend or vendor portal): once the untaxed goods reach it the price becomes 0 (shown "Free").
5. Confirm the order: company order keeps only the platform part, each vendor order its own delivery line; confirmation page, quotation PDF and (with vendor-only presentation on) the marketplace confirmation email list the breakdown.

## LIMITATIONS
- Threshold lives on each zone row (per governorate), not one value for a whole vendor.
- Threshold basis is untaxed amount of the goods of that scope (platform: company + FBL lines, non-service; vendor: that vendor's FBM lines). Discounts included, delivery and taxes excluded.
- The breakdown text uses store name (or name) of the vendor; existing translations of the .pot are regenerated by the coordinator.
- Only the marketplace confirmation email and the sale PDF got the breakdown; the vendor emails (vendor order confirmed etc.) are untouched.
- Non-zone carriers: no breakdown (empty list).

## DECISIONS
- Data model: dedicated `is_platform` flag with empty vendor_id instead of the company's partner: the vendor record rule/portal queries filter `vendor_id = own`, so platform rows are automatically invisible to vendors with no rule change, no fake "vendor" partner needed (the vendor domain requires is_marketplace_vendor) and multi-company partner issues are avoided.
- Platform price is charged ONCE if the cart has company products (non-service) and/or FBL lines. FBL goods are priced with the platform price only (the FBL vendor's own zone price is no longer added) since the platform ships them. FBM-only carts are byte-for-byte unchanged.
- Split: FBM vendors get their own zone price (recomputed with the free-shipping rule, same error if a vendor has no price). The platform part is the remainder of the checkout's delivery line, so it stays on the company order (no platform zone lookup at the split, so an old cart priced before this fix still confirms). When the checkout has no company product left (FBL-only or FBL + FBM vendors), `fbl_delivery_income` decides: "vendor" (default) allocates the remainder to the FBL vendors' orders in proportion to their untaxed FBL amount (rounding on the last), "platform" posts it as platform delivery income (existing accounting call). With company products the platform part always stays on the company order (before the fix vendor mode paid the FBL vendor its own price even then).
- Breakdown after the split is read from the actual delivery lines (company order + vendor orders) so it always matches the money; before the split it comes from the zone plan.
- Templates via inheritance: `website_sale.total` (cart/checkout/payment), `website_sale.confirmation`, `sale.report_saleorder_document`; mail body is a noupdate record so edited + migration.
