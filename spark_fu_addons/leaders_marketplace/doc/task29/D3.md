## SETTINGS (Settings > Marketplace > Product Questions & Answers)
- `leaders_marketplace.product_questions_enabled` Boolean, default False = master switch. OFF: no section on the product page, all routes 404, no vendor nav entry.
- `product_questions_moderation` Boolean, default False. OFF: an answered question is public. ON: an answered question also needs a reviewer approval.
- `product_questions_daily_limit` Integer, default 5 (<=0 treated as 5): max questions per user per 24 h.
- Notification events `notify_vendor_question_asked`, `notify_customer_question_answered` (seeded True in data; pre-migration seeds False on upgraded DBs).

## ROUTES_API
- POST /shop/product/<template_id>/question (csrf, auth public route): body `question`, optional `product_id`. Anonymous -> redirect to /web/login?redirect=<product url>. Unknown/unpublished/not sale_ok -> 404. Success or refusal -> redirect to `<product url>?lm_q=<ok|empty|long|limit|duplicate>#lm_questions` (page maps the code to a message; no free text in the URL).
- GET /my/vendor/questions?filter=unanswered(default)|answered|all: cards of the questions on templates where the vendor has an ACTIVE offer (state active, active True), answer form for editable ones. `_require_vendor` (terms check), frozen vendors allowed (like disputes).
- POST /my/vendor/questions/<id>/answer (csrf, field `answer`): owner or staff. Not found 404; no active offer on the template -> 403; hidden/empty/too long/answered by another vendor -> redirect with ?error=. Success -> ?saved=1.
## UAT
1. Enable "Product Questions & Answers" in Settings (optionally moderation, daily limit).
2. Customer logged in opens a published product: "Questions & answers" section under the description, asks a question: green message; own pending question is listed. Anonymous: only a "Log in to ask a question" button.
3. Vendor with an active offer on the product (owner or staff): sidebar "Questions" appears; opens it, answers; mail to vendor team was queued at ask time (if the event is enabled).
4. Product page now shows question + answer + store name + date (with moderation on: after Marketplace > Operations > Product Questions > filter "Questions to moderate" > Approve Answer). The customer gets the "answered" mail when the answer becomes public (once).
5. Reviewer hides a question (form button, optional reason): gone from the product page; vendor sees "hidden" and cannot edit; "Show Again" restores.
6. Switch off: section gone, /my/vendor/questions and both POSTs are 404, no nav entry.
## LIMITATIONS
Asker name shown to the vendor is the first word only; the public page never shows the asker. Duplicate/limit guard is not atomic under truly concurrent requests. Unhide clears hidden_by/date/reason (no history, model has no chatter). A vendor cannot overwrite an answer given by another vendor. Variant is not chosen on the form (product_id only via optional param).
## DECISIONS
Public only when answered in all modes; `approved` set True automatically at answer time when moderation is off, so turning moderation on later keeps old answers public; editing an answer under moderation returns it to review. Customer mail is sent when the answer becomes public (not before approval). Flash messages are codes in the query string (no injected text). Text stored raw (control chars stripped) and always escaped with t-esc. Vendor routes are in portal.py to reuse `_require_vendor`; the customer route is in its own controller.
