## UAT
1. Marketplace > Configuration > Delivery Zones: set Min/Max days on a platform row (Cairo 1-2) and on a vendor row (3-5).
2. Vendor owner: /my/vendor/delivery-zones, fill "Days from/to" in the add form or a row, Save.
3. Website cart/checkout/payment with the zone carrier, ship to Cairo, company product + that vendor's product: under the delivery totals "Leaders: Arrives in 1–2 days", "<Store>: Arrives in 3–5 days". Sellers/zones without days show nothing.
4. Confirm: same lines on the confirmation page; customer vendor-order page shows the seller's estimate; with Unified My Orders on, the checkout detail shows it in the company section and in each seller section.
5. Clear the days: nothing shown anywhere. Other carriers: nothing. Check en/ar.
## LIMITATIONS
Estimate is per zone (governorate), not per product. Not in the order email/PDF. Vendor order page for an FBL line shows the platform zone's estimate labelled "Leaders". Arabic text needs the coordinator's i18n regeneration (ar test checks structure only).
