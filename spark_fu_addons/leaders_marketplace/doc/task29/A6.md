## UAT
1. FBM order of 4 pieces. Vendor portal > order page: the Ship form lists each product with "available / ordered"; set 1, enter tracking A, Ship: group "Partially Shipped", vendor order "Partially Fulfilled", delivery A done and a backorder delivery (same group) waiting.
2. Ship again (tracking B is required, must differ per shipment): the group becomes "Shipped" (tracking column "A, B"), order "Fulfilled"; then Mark as Delivered works as before.
3. Backend: vendor order > Fulfillment tab: "Ship Quantities" opens the wizard (ordered/available/to ship, tracking ref); "Ship" (old button) ships everything left; "Cancel Remaining" (partially shipped only) cancels the open backorder and the group becomes Shipped with what left.
4. FBL: warehouse validates the delivery with fewer quantities and Odoo's "Create backorder": group turns Partially Shipped, order Partially Fulfilled; validating the backorder completes it.
5. Stock short (5 in stock, 3 reserved by another order, order of 4): "Ship" of everything says not enough stock; ship 2 via quantities works.
6. Customer cancel buttons disappear after the first parcel ("Request a return after delivery"); returns can be requested only for shipped quantities.

## LIMITATIONS
- Delivery flow (report delivery / delivered / customer confirm / dispute) works only on a fully shipped group: a partially shipped group waits for the rest (or "Cancel Remaining").
- Escrow/earnings unchanged: created at completion (all groups delivered) or at `fulfilled` for the on_ship policy (never at partially_fulfilled).
- Customer "shipped" email is sent when the group becomes fully shipped (not per partial parcel). The portal shows one tracking number field per shipment; the customer page still shows the group's joined tracking.
- Cancel Remaining does not resize the vendor order amounts/earning: money for the unshipped part must be handled with the usual refund/return tools (out of scope).
- The vendor cancel / customer cancel / COD refusal are refused for partially shipped groups (something already left).
- The portal form and the wizard show only lines that still have an open move; several open backorders per line are summed.

## DECISIONS
- Group state `partially_shipped` is in `_SHIPPED_GROUP_STATES` (so cancel guards, customer-cancel scopes and returns treat it as "shipped") but the vendor order becomes `fulfilled` only if no group is partially shipped (else partially_fulfilled), and delivery/completion require `shipped`.
- Odoo's standard backorder creation is used (move.quantity + picked then `_action_done`, no cancel_backorder); the group/line links are copied to the backorder by overriding `_create_backorder_picking` and `_prepare_move_split_vals`, so FBL warehouse validation with the Odoo backorder dialog lands in the same group through the existing `_sync_from_pickings` hook.
- After the first shipment an explicit tracking reference is required for FBM (each picking its own).
- COD refusal at the door acts only on a fully `shipped` group; partially shipped groups (own or sibling) are refused.
- `quantities` keys are vendor order line ids (unambiguous vs move ids).
