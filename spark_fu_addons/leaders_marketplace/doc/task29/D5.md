## DECISIONS
- Categories: shipping (order_shipped, order_delivered), orders (order_cancelled, order_cancelled_by_customer), returns (return_approved, return_rejected, return_refunded), questions (question_answered).

- Preference read from the recipient's commercial partner (a company's contacts share it); saved on the user's commercial partner. `force=True` and mandatory events ignore it; skipped mails are logged at debug.
- Vendor/security mails untouched (note on the page).
