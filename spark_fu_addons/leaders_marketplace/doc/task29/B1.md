## UAT
1. Settings > Marketplace: tick "Vendor Product Discounts" (threshold 50%).
2. Vendor owner > Products & Offers > Edit: "Discount" card: 20% no dates -> active at once; the list shows a -20% badge. Website: product page shows the original price struck, the new price and "-20%"; shop grid and cart likewise.
3. Set 70%: "waits for approval"; backend Marketplace > Operations > Discounts to Review: Approve/Reject buttons. Price stays regular until approved.
4. Set start in the future: Scheduled; cron (or wait <=15 min) activates it; an end date expires it and the Buy Box is re-ranked.
5. Product list: tick several offers, percent (+dates) > Apply discount; "Remove discount" clears.
6. Vendor order/invoice: line shows unit price (original), Disc.% and the discounted subtotal; commission uses the subtotal.

## LIMITATIONS
- The website "-X%" is floored and measured from the 30-day reference price; the cart line / vendor invoice carry the real discount from the REGULAR price. In the price-raised-before-promotion case product page and grid show no badge, while the cart line still shows the real line discount.
- Badge on the product page only for single-variant products (a variant switch does not refresh a server-rendered badge); the struck price works for all.
- Sorting/filtering of the shop by price uses list_price (regular).
- Pricelist + vendor discount are chained, not added: paid = price x (1 - vendor%) x (1 - pricelist%).
- Backend form shows discount fields read-only; only the vendor (portal/import/API) sets them; reviewers can approve/reject/remove.
- Price history dates begin at the upgrade for pre-existing offers.

## DECISIONS
- `offer.price` = regular price; `template.list_price` keeps syncing to the winner's REGULAR price so backend-created lines (priced from list_price, offer attached at confirmation) never double-discount; the cart line keeps the regular price_unit + `discount` (vendor % chained with the pricelist's), so vendor order lines/invoices show both and commission (price_subtotal) uses sold amounts (verified by tests).
- Website price = effective price via `leaders_offer_unit_price` context in `_get_additionnal_combination_info`; grid scales the regular price by effective/regular and reference/regular.
- A sale price no longer below a lowered regular price is cleared; raising the regular price of an approved price-type discount re-checks the threshold.
- Cron also refreshes the discount of open (draft) cart lines; checkout revalidation reports a discount change.
